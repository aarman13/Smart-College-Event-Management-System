About HMV

Hans Raj Mahila Maha Vidyalaya, Jalandhar is a premier institution of North India providing value-based, employment-oriented quality education to women.

Founded in 1927 in Lahore by Mahatma Hans Raj Ji, a dedicated reformer and staunch advocate of the DAV Movement, Hans Raj Mahila Maha Vidyalaya stands tall as a beacon of women’s education and empowerment in North India. After Partition in 1948, the institution was relocated to Jalandhar, where it continued its noble mission of educating and empowering women. A landmark in its journey was the inauguration of its core block in 1959 by Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan, then Vice President of India, a testimony to its growing stature in the field of education.

Today, under the visionary leadership of Padma Shri Awardee Dr. Punam Suri, President, DAV College Managing Committee, New Delhi, HMV has evolved into a premier institution dedicated to value-based, employment-oriented education. The college proudly holds an ‘A++’ grade with a score of 3.65 in the third cycle of NAAC Reaccreditation, a distinction that reflects its commitment to academic excellence. Recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) as a "College of Excellence," HMV serves over 5,000 students, providing them with a holistic learning experience beyond conventional academics.

Our Core Values

Vision

Women Education Focusing on Holistic Development and Empowerment.

Mission

Value Oriented, Skill Based and Globally Competent Education in sync with Nation’s March towards Sustainable Growth and Prosperity.

Belief

To impart the kind of education that enables women to become responsible, self-sufficient, independent and empathetic global citizens, who can bring a positive change and make a sustainable contribution to this universe.
